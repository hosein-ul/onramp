Sheet 4 · Nothing to take on trust

No login. No server. Just the chain.

Waypoint has no database. A receipt carries only pointers; the page that opens it reads the chain again and shows what the chain says.

  • A transaction that lands but fails is never counted.
  • Progress is recomputed on every visit.
  • Test funds have no value; network rules may change.
