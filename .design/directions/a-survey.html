Nothing to take on trust

The map is the territory.

Waypoint has no server, no database and no login. A receipt carries only pointers; the page that opens it reads the chain again and shows what the chain says.

  • A transaction that lands but fails is never counted.
  • Progress is recomputed from on-chain data on every visit.
  • Test funds have no value, and network rules may change.
